Why a mouse?

Topo is Italian for mouse. Topo Gigio was a puppet on The Ed Sullivan Show in the 1960s, and a cartoon and comic-book character in Italy.

Frank, who founded the restaurant, watched him as a boy. When he came to the United States from Calabria he brought his family's recipes with him, and they are still on the menu.
